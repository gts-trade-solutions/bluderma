ANALYTICS TOOL RECOMMENDATION - BluDerma
26 August 2026


RECOMMENDED

1. Cube (cube.dev)  -  for doctor-facing analytics
   - Each doctor sees only their own data (built-in security)
   - Charts built inside the portal, matching BluDerma's design
   - Fast even as data grows
   - Works directly with our existing MySQL database

2. PostHog  -  for understanding how the platform is used
   - Funnels and drop-off reports
   - Session replay (see how doctors actually use the portal)
   - A/B testing
   - Can be self-hosted, so data stays with us


QUICKER ALTERNATIVE

Metabase  -  ready-made dashboards, no coding, open source.
Faster to launch, but looks like a separate tool rather than
part of BluDerma. Good as a first step.


NOT ADVISED

Google Looker Studio, Mixpanel, Amplitude - data must leave our
servers, and per-doctor separation is weak. Not suitable for
health-related data.


IMPORTANT

No patient names, photos or clinical notes should ever go to an
analytics tool. Only doctor-level totals and internal IDs.


NOTE

BluDerma already calculates revenue, clinic ranking, equipment
payback and profit correctly in its own code. An analytics tool
adds charts and exploration on top of this - it does not replace it.
