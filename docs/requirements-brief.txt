===============================================================================
1.  GLOBAL RULES  -  these override any module spec below
===============================================================================
G-1  NO PRICES on treatment or deal cards. Ever. Treatment name only.
G-2  NO CLINIC NAME and NO BRANCH/LOCATION on treatment or deal cards.
G-3  Prices ARE shown in exactly three places, and nowhere else:
       - the two analyzers (strike-through offer, G-4)
       - doctor consultation fees (C-26)
       - CME subscription (E-5)
     Everything else is enquiry-first; final cost is given in consultation.
G-4  Analyzer pricing, shown as strike-through + FREE:
       SKIN analyzer   Rs.100  ->  FREE
       HAIR analyzer   Rs.50   ->  FREE      (hair analyzer does not exist yet)
G-5  Region selector is global (every page). Order: CITIES first, STATES second.
G-6  Two audiences, two portals: CLIENT (Sec 2) and DOCTOR (Sec 3).
     Sec 4-6 (CME / Inventory / Setup Clinic) are shared or doctor-facing.


===============================================================================
2.  CLIENT PORTAL
===============================================================================

--- 2.1  Home page -----------------------------------------------------------
C-1   Client home page is the marketplace hub (not the analyzer).
C-2   Hero/front page modelled on curology.com: oversized headline, supporting
      line, proof tiles, one primary CTA, full-bleed photograph.
C-3   Running/scrolling banner, marked "COMING SOON":
      "Consult 2000-mile skincare specialists within 20 minutes."
      (verbatim: "consult 2000miles skin care specialist within 20 mins")
C-4   Navbar carries an "ANALYZE YOUR SKIN" button. Client side only.
C-5   Navbar carries the region dropdown (G-5).

--- 2.2  Categories ----------------------------------------------------------
C-10  Category row, small pill style (unni.app). Named categories required:
        Lifting | Skin | Botox | Waxing | Filler | Eye Surgery | Nose Surgery
C-11  Plus all categories from curology.com.
C-12  Plus all categories from tamiralife.com (R-4 list is a good start).
C-13  Plus all categories from the asthetiq reference -- BLOCKED, see R-5.
C-14  Hot Deals and All Deals both sit UNDER the category structure, i.e. a
      category page shows its own hot deals and all deals.

--- 2.3  Deals ---------------------------------------------------------------
C-15  HOT DEALS  - visually attractive. Badges, urgency, colour, motion.
C-16  ALL DEALS  - deliberately plain. No badges, no urgency, no decoration.
C-17  Both obey G-1 and G-2: treatment name only, no price, no clinic, no branch.

--- 2.4  Know Yourself  (was unni's "column") --------------------------------
C-20  Rename unni.app's editorial "column" concept to "KNOW YOURSELF".
C-21  Behaves like a blog, presented like a magazine - issues/features, not a
      reverse-chronological feed.
C-22  Each article is tied to a service/treatment.

--- 2.5  Before & After ------------------------------------------------------
C-23  Dedicated Before & After section on the client side.
C-24  Client before/after images also appear inside the doctor block (C-26).

--- 2.6  Doctor discovery block ----------------------------------------------
C-25  A section presenting doctors. Contains, in order:
C-26    - Interested doctors (doctors who responded / are available)
C-27    - Doctor pricing (consultation fee - allowed under G-3)
C-28    - Client before/after images
C-29    - Usual available time slots
C-30    - Payment gateway

--- 2.7  Rx Skin -------------------------------------------------------------
C-31  "RX SKIN" section listing skin CONDITIONS.
C-32  Condition list sourced from curology.com (R-2b) and the asthetiq
      reference (BLOCKED, R-5).
C-33  Hovering a skin-condition category reveals a ONE-LINE description.

--- 2.8  Get Expert Advice  (the main conversion flow) -----------------------
C-34  "GET EXPERT ADVICE" section sits directly BELOW Rx Skin.
C-35  Clicking it opens a MODAL containing a form. Modal pattern and field set
      both taken from curology.com (R-2c) - use all their inputs.
C-36  Inside the form, a "TELL US ABOUT YOUR SKIN" step:
        - if the client HAS already run the skin analyzer -> their report is
          pulled in automatically, and is OPTIONAL to review
        - if they have NOT -> offer to run the analyzer, then return them to
          this form at the same step (do not lose entered data)
C-37  On form completion, offer:
        (a) pick appointment DATE + TIME
        (b) INSTANT appointment
        (c) BOOK PROCEDURE - domestic and international, in-clinic
            -> this routes the client to the Hot Deals page
C-38  Also offer: suggested clinics NEAR the client, and a HOME VISIT option.
C-39  Terms & Conditions wording modelled on practo.com (R-3).

--- 2.9  Help Us To Know You -------------------------------------------------
C-40  A separate page, reached by its own button: "HELP US TO KNOW YOU".
C-41  Purpose: collect client profile information up front.
      >>> Overlaps heavily with C-35. See Q-4.

     does not?

     prices anywhere. This brief introduces doctor pricing (C-27), a payment
     gateway (C-30) and a CME subscription (E-5). Confirm: deals and treatments
     stay price-free (G-1), but consultations and procedures become payable.

Q-6  Speaker form (E-2) - which fields are mandatory?

Q-7  Confirm the AEO scope in A-2.

Q-8  HAIR ANALYZER (G-4) does not exist. Is it a second vendor integration, or
     the same engine in a hair mode? Who supplies it?

Q-9  Payment gateway (C-30) - which provider? Razorpay / Stripe / PayU?
     International bookings (C-37c) imply multi-currency.

Q-10 "Consult 2000-mile specialists within 20 minutes" (C-3) - confirm the
     wording. Is this teleconsult with a distance-based promise, or a
     marketing phrase only?

Q-11 Rs.100 for the skin analyzer (G-4) vs the Rs.99 currently on the page.
     Confirm Rs.100 is intended.
